Why Pakistani Shoppers Overpay

E-commerce in Pakistan is fragmented across hundreds of isolated store websites. The same gadget, shirt, or appliance often sells with price differences of Rs. 3,000 to Rs. 25,000 between stores like PriceOye, Daraz Mall, Telemart, and Mega.pk.

PennyWise solves this by deploying autonomous web extraction that indexes live merchant pages, extracts the actual checkout PKR price, and highlights the cheapest verified store without sponsored bias.
