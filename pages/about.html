About PennyWise

PennyWise addresses the fragmentation in Pakistani e-commerce by indexing live prices directly from store checkout pages. We eliminate synthetic estimates, rank verified lowest prices first, and connect users straight to the merchant.

The Problem & Solution Why We Built This

What's the Need :

Online shopping in Pakistan has grown exponentially, but with that growth came chaos. Anyone who has ever tried buying a new smartphone, laptop, air conditioner, or gaming console knows the exact headache: you end up opening dozens of browser tabs—Daraz Mall, PriceOye, Telemart, Shophive, Paklap, and Mega.pk—mindlessly hopping back and forth, squinting at different model numbers, calculating hidden delivery charges, and wondering if that "50% off" discount is even real.

End Mindless Searching

Instead of wasting 45 minutes manually hunting across multiple stores, get a comprehensive cross-merchant comparison in one instant search.

Expose Hidden Price Gaps

Identical products often differ by Rs. 5,000 to Rs. 40,000 between stores. We sort listings strictly from lowest to highest so your savings are guaranteed.

Verified Checkout Prices

No fake slashed prices or bait-and-switch advertising. We index actual shelf and checkout numbers from Pakistan's most trusted retailers.

Core Principles

  • Directly extract live product prices in PKR across certified Pakistani merchants.
  • Ascending deal ranking: the lowest verified price always receives the #1 badge.
  • Strict accuracy policy: drop listings that lack confirmed retail numbers.
  • Maintain a fast, accessible, lightweight frontend interface.
Verified Pakistan Store Index

Integrity Standards

  • No paid ranking positions: commercial placement cannot alter price-based sorting.
  • Zero synthetic data: no machine-generated or estimated prices.
  • Transparent outlinks: all links point directly to the merchant product URL.
  • Multi-sector indexing: gadgets, home appliances, apparel, and transit tickets.

A Note From The Creators

PennyWise wasn't crafted in a corporate boardroom or financed by venture capital. It was proudly dreamed up, designed, and built by three university students in Pakistan who simply wanted a smarter way to shop.

We watched our families, friends, and fellow hostel roommates repeatedly overpay for laptops, textbooks, phones, and appliances simply because they didn't have the time or patience to check every single Pakistani e-commerce store one by one. We knew technology could fix this—not with bloated ads or sponsored bias, but with a fast, transparent platform that always puts the shopper first.
