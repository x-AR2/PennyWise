About PennyWise

PennyWise addresses the fragmentation in Pakistani e-commerce by indexing live prices directly from store checkout pages. We eliminate synthetic estimates, rank verified lowest prices first, and connect users straight to the merchant.

Core Principles

  • Directly extract live product prices in PKR across certified Pakistani merchants.
  • Ascending deal ranking: the lowest verified price always receives the #1 badge.
  • Strict accuracy policy: drop listings that lack confirmed retail numbers.
  • Maintain a fast, accessible, lightweight frontend interface.
Verified Pakistan Store Index

Integrity Standards

  • No paid ranking positions: commercial placement cannot alter price-based sorting.
  • Zero synthetic data: no machine-generated or estimated prices.
  • Transparent outlinks: all links point directly to the merchant product URL.
  • Multi-sector indexing: gadgets, home appliances, apparel, and transit tickets.
