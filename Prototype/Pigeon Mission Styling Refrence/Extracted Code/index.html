The story

It started with a sketchbook.

One day, Logan approached his dad with an idea. He wanted to create his own game.

As an avid fan of Super Mario, Yoshi’s Island and other titles, the young gamer wanted to develop his OWN adventure. So, he ran to his sketchbook to jot down his ideas and concepts.

His dad JUMPED at the idea and got to work! Together, they transformed Logan’s imagination into a captivating digital world, where players can explore, fight, and embark on an unforgettable journey.

The game

Seven worlds.Eight bosses.One flightless pigeon.

A pixel-rich platformer born from a six-year-old’s imagination. Skill-based gameplay that is fun for kids and rewarding for seasoned platformer fans: precision controls, awesome power-ups, fierce boss fights and an epic storyline.

Story

Save Mom & Dad!

Meet Archie, a brave flightless pigeon from Pepper Pines. One night while he was sleeping, his whole family was captured by Crazy Guy, a goo-mutated villain with big plans. Now Archie is on a mission to save his mom and dad.

Wall-jump, dash and battle through seven dreamy worlds inspired by Logan’s original drawings. Unlock quirky gadgets, power up with the Harvest Pulse System, and uncover heartfelt story beats and laugh-out-loud boss fights along the way.
